### 🏠 About Me:

With over 9 years of experience spanning full-stack web development, secure cloud infrastructure, and high-performance data processing pipelines, I specialize in translating complex domain requirements into secure, highly observable, and computationally reproducible data platforms.

🔑 **Key Engineering Focus:**
* ⚙️ **Systems & Backend:** Deep foundation in C++, Python, Java, and JavaScript/TypeScript, constructing robust high-throughput telemetry pathways and event-driven architectures.
